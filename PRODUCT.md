# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitor is the owner of a personal technology collection, or a reader invited to browse it. They arrive to scan a curated catalogue, search for a device, and compare configurations without wading through a generic product-card wall.

## Product Purpose

TECH ARSENAL is a personal digital collection catalogue. It records owned devices and pursued devices across categories, exposing the device identity, status, concise configuration, and longer personal rationale. Success is fast discovery and comparison while retaining the sense that the collection has been deliberately edited.

## Positioning

Unlike a store catalogue, every entry is a numbered personal acquisition or aspiration with an ownership state and an editorial reason for inclusion.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse five device chapters, filter by category, search device and brand names, open an entry for its fuller specification record, and can use the separate comparison surface for selected devices.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript are the implementation stack.
- The page must preserve its real collection entries, product images, category filters, search behavior, and accessible keyboard dismissal of details.
- The requested redesign prioritizes efficient browsing, search, and configuration comparison.
- Each category should be led by one visually prominent featured device; remaining entries must remain easy to scan.

## Brand Commitments

- The page name is TECH ARSENAL.
- The intended visual reference is a high-end product annual, not a gaming or neon-cyber interface.
- Existing ownership and wishlist terminology, lot numbering, and personal collection voice remain meaningful content.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing page: `tech-arsenal.html`.
- Shared visual styles: `arsenal-pages.css`.
- Existing device images: `assets/images/`.
- Existing comparison behavior: `arsenal-compare.js` and `comparison-lab.html`.
- No new factual product claims or external imagery are authorized for this redesign.

## Product Principles

- Make the catalogue scannable before making it decorative.
- Let real device data, editorial hierarchy, and ownership state carry the page.
- Give every chapter a clear lead object without burying the rest of the collection.
- Preserve useful interaction and replace only the visual presentation that obstructs it.
